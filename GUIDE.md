# Meet Puff 🌸 — your one-page guide

Puff is a little lavender cloud who lives in the corner of your screen, keeps you
company, and helps you focus. Here's everything in one page.

## Where is it?
Bottom-right of your screen, floating on top of your other windows, plus an icon
in your **menu bar / system tray**. Closing the pet doesn't quit it — use the
tray's **Quit Puff**.

## How to play with Puff
| Do this | Puff does |
|---|---|
| **Hover** over it | hops, waves, eyes follow your cursor |
| **Single-click** | squishes, hearts, sometimes a cute line (occasionally your name 💜) |
| **Double-click** | opens/closes the focus panel |
| **Click + drag** | pick it up and move it anywhere; drop near an edge to snap it |
| **Pet it** — rub the mouse back-and-forth over Puff | heart eyes + purring 🥰 |
| **Right-click** | quick menu: Start focus · Add task · Hide 30 min · Settings · Quit |

Leave it alone a while and it may **wander** along the bottom of the screen.
Step away for 3 minutes and it **falls asleep**, then greets you when you're back.

## Feelings
Puff shows real moods: happy, love, surprised, wink, giggle, **pouty**, sleepy,
and celebrating. During a **focus session** it puts on tiny glasses and types at
a little laptop right next to you — it won't wander or nap while you work.

## Getting focused
Double-click to open the panel:
- **Pomodoro timer** — 25 min focus / 5 min break, with a longer break every 4
  rounds (all adjustable in Settings).
- **Tasks** — add, tick off (Puff celebrates!), and **drag to reorder**.
  Unfinished tasks roll over to tomorrow; finished ones clear at midnight.
- **Streak** — do at least one focus session a day and Puff earns accessories:
  ⭐ 3 days · 🎀 7 · 👑 14 · glowing crown 👑✨ 30.

**Hotkeys:** `⌘/Ctrl + Shift + P` toggles the panel · `⌘/Ctrl + Shift + F`
starts/pauses focus (change them in Settings).

## Looking after you
Puff can nudge you to drink water, stretch, rest your eyes (20-20-20), and wind
down late at night — every reminder is on/off with its own timing in **Settings**.
It automatically goes **quiet** during full-screen apps, or when you choose
"Pause reminders for 1 hour" from the tray. Optionally, it can give a gentle
"back to it?" if you drift onto distracting sites during focus (off by default;
window titles are only ever checked in memory, never saved).

## Settings
Open from the tray or right-click menu. Set what Puff calls you, its size,
timer lengths, reminders, wandering, do-not-disturb, launch-at-login, hotkeys,
and how chatty it is. Everything applies instantly.

## Talk to Puff
Open the panel and type in the **"talk to puff…"** box. Without any setup Puff
replies with cute canned lines. For real conversation, add an **Anthropic API
key** in Settings (from console.anthropic.com — pay-as-you-go, *not* a Claude
subscription; Haiku is the cheapest model).

## Break time = playtime
Start a break and Puff gets playful — it wanders around and, depending on how
long your break is, will **dance**, **take a coffee**, **meditate**, or **nap**,
and sometimes asks if it can do something for you. Tick a task and you'll hear a
little **celebration chime** 🎵 (toggle in Settings).

## Keeping you honest
Open a social-media site and Puff pops onto your screen with an **angry face**
😤 (toggleable). It also floats above *everything* — Chrome, VS Code, your
terminal, even full-screen apps — and gives a little wave at what you're working
on. It never disappears on its own; hide it from the tray if you need quiet.

*Tip: run with `PUFF_DEBUG=1 npm start` to see logs while tinkering.*
